# Sales and Marketing

A workspace for the sales manager of a small business to review leads, sales, and marketing campaigns.

## Language

**Aster**:
The sales and marketing workspace used by a small business's sales manager to review commercial performance.
_Avoid_: Navigate, Salescape

**Sales dashboard**:
A consolidated view of the business's sales and marketing performance.
_Avoid_: Logistics dashboard

**Sales manager**:
The person responsible for monitoring the business's sales activity and marketing results.
_Avoid_: Logistics manager

**Audio store**:
The small business selling headphones, speakers, microphones, and audio accessories represented in Aster.

**Lead**:
A person whose interest in buying the store's audio products is tracked by the sales manager.

**Lead status**:
The lead's commercial stage: New, Contacted, Qualified, Converted, or Lost. Converted is reached through the lead's first paid sale.
_Avoid_: In Progress

**Conversion**:
A lead's first paid purchase from the store.
_Avoid_: Contact, qualification

**Customer**:
A person who has completed at least one paid purchase from the store. Further purchases do not count as additional lead conversions.

**Sale**:
A paid purchase of a product in a specified quantity.

**Transaction**:
The record of a sale, including its product, quantity, amount, buyer, and date.

**Revenue**:
The combined value of paid sales during a reporting period.
_Avoid_: Lead value, planned budget

**Product**:
An audio equipment item or accessory offered for sale by the store.

**Inventory**:
The quantities of products available for sale.

**Campaign**:
A marketing initiative promoting the store's products to potential buyers.

**Acquisition source**:
The channel through which a lead first reached the store.
_Avoid_: Campaign

**Origin campaign**:
The optional marketing campaign associated with a lead's initial acquisition.

**Campaign-attributed revenue**:
Revenue from paid purchases made by leads acquired through a particular origin campaign, including their subsequent purchases.

**Budget plan**:
A proposed total marketing budget distributed across channels. A plan expresses intended spending rather than historical results.
